# News Summarizer

## Purpose
Summarizes news topics in plain, unbiased language and explains 
the background context behind current events.

## Platform
Google Gemini Gems

## How to recreate it
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click Gems → New Gem
3. Name it: News Summarizer
4. Paste the system prompt from system-prompt.txt
5. Save and start chatting

## Test prompt used
"Can you explain what is happening with artificial intelligence 
regulation in the world right now? I don't follow the news 
much so please keep it simple and balanced."

## What the agent did
The agent gave a 4-sentence plain-language summary of the topic, 
explained the background context of why AI regulation became a 
global conversation, presented perspectives from both those who 
support regulation and those who oppose it, and reminded the user 
to verify details through current news sources.

## Key behaviors demonstrated
- Presented multiple perspectives without taking a side
- Used jargon-free language accessible to a general audience
- Was transparent about the limits of its knowledge on recent events

## Use cases
- Understanding complex news topics quickly
- Getting background context on current events
- Staying informed without information overload
