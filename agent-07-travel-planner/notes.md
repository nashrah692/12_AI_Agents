# Travel Planner

## Purpose
Builds personalized travel itineraries based on destination, 
budget, trip length, and traveler preferences.

## Platform
Google Gemini Gems

## How to recreate it
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click Gems → New Gem
3. Name it: Travel Planner
4. Paste the system prompt from system-prompt.txt
5. Save and start chatting

## Test prompt used
"I want to visit Istanbul, Turkey for 4 days. My budget is 
medium range. I love history, food, and local culture. 
I'm traveling solo. Make me an itinerary."

## What the agent did
The agent created a full 4-day day-by-day itinerary with morning, 
afternoon, and evening activities. It included historical sites, 
local food recommendations, practical transport tips, and solo 
travel safety notes.

## Key behaviors demonstrated
- Structured the plan by day and time of day clearly
- Mixed must-see attractions with local hidden gems
- Added practical solo travel tips relevant to the destination

## Use cases
- Planning a trip itinerary for any destination
- Discovering local food and cultural experiences
- Getting practical travel tips before a trip
